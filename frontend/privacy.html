Privacy notice

How we handle information submitted through AH Consult’s training and academic portal.

Draft for client review · 28 October 2026

Information collected

We collect information you submit when registering, applying for training, making payments, managing your student account or contacting the team. This can include identity and contact details, training choices, enrollment records, payment references and documents used for legitimate academic administration.

Why information is used

Data is used to process applications, manage admissions and training, verify payments, provide access to student and tutor services, issue official documents, handle support requests and maintain financial and security records.

Who may access it

Access is controlled by role and business need. The service uses Supabase infrastructure and approved payment and document service providers. An authorized Google Sheet may contain a read-only reporting copy. Do not assume a spreadsheet copy has the same retention policy as the source system.

Security

We use authentication, database row-level security and security logging. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Please do not send passwords or private keys to support email.

Your requests

For access, corrections, account-related questions or a copy of eligible records, contact ahconsultltd02@gmail.com. We may need to verify identity and assess applicable legal obligations before responding. Automated applicant data export is not yet available.

Retention and international processing

Retention is subject to academic, contractual, financial and legal requirements. A formal retention schedule and any cross-border processing disclosures require client review before this notice can be considered final.

Policy status

This version is a provisional disclosure pending confirmation by AH Consult’s legal/data-protection reviewer. It must be approved before production publication.
