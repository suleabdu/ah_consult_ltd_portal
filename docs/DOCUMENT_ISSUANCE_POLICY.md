# AHCT document issuance policy — implementation vs institutional approval

| Document | Database gate and issuer | Current scope | Institution policy still required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Admission Letter | Approved admission; generated from approved student record | Approved admission only | Authorized admission officer and correction process |
| Payment Receipt | Verified ledger, expected amount covered, finance-verified manual settlement OR signed provider confirmation | Institutional acknowledgment; not an external bank-issued receipt | Finance fraud review, refunds/reversals |
| Deferment Approval Letter | Approved deferment record | Approved deferment(s) | Maximum duration, return-to-study policy |
| Statement of Results | Published reviewed results; academic officer review; independent registrar authorization | May be partial, states actual published scope | Definitions of provisional/final statement; grading appeal periods |
| Final Transcript | Every non-deferred course has reviewed published grades; academic review + separate registrar approval | Full enrolled course list for that application | Cohort requirements, exemptions, transcript seal/award language |
| Certificate | Denied pending formal completion and award rules | SAMPLE PREVIEW ONLY | Approved versioned course requirements, passing thresholds, attendance, projects, award authority and signature service |

No one should grant themselves a certificate by editing a published template. Registrar approval is an authorization, not proof that all AHCT award rules have been approved. Existing archived PDFs are retained unchanged. When a grade or enrolment is edited, all current academic review signoffs on that application are invalidated and must be re-reviewed. Future additions should include structured award policy versions, verified signatory authorization dates, revocation/replacement workflows and a final accredited-award review (if external accreditation is actually held).
